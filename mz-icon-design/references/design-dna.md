# MZ Design DNA

Use `quiet`, `human`, `digital`, and `crafted` as the shared MZ identity. Express one concept per icon, prioritise visual balance over literal completeness, and reuse proportions only inside the current MZ set.

## Categories

- `core`: restrained navigation and action concepts.
- `system`: technical or product concepts with one meaningful structural detail.
- `life`: reflective personal concepts with an approachable metaphor.
- `brand`: MZ-specific concepts only when the user supplies the required identity reference; do not invent logos or third-party marks.

## Originality boundary

Use only original geometry, metaphors, prompts, scripts, and assets. The external oil-icon and voxel-icon projects inform the separation of small UI vectors from larger coherent spot-icon sets; never copy their files, icons, construction recipes, palette registries, or wording. Never add third-party brand marks unless supplied and authorised by the user.
